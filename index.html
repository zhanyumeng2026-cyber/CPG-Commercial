为什么选择快消Why FMCG

01

离消费者更近Closer to real consumers

在互联网，我通过数据认识用户；在快消，每一次购买都是真金白银的选择。复购，是对品牌最诚实的评价。In tech, I got to know users through dashboards. In FMCG, every purchase is someone spending their own money. A repeat purchase is the most honest review a brand can get.

02

内容越多，品牌越重要More content, more need for brands

我亲手做过 AI 内容生产，知道内容可以又快又多；但也正因如此，被记住变得更难。清晰的定位和有温度的表达，才会让人停下来，然后再次选择。I've built AI content tools, so I know how fast and cheap content has become. That's exactly why being remembered is harder than ever. A clear point of view and a genuine voice are what make people stop — and come back.

03

对完整的生意负责Owning the whole business

过去我常负责链路中的一环。快消的品牌和 CBD 经理，要对洞察、产品、传播、渠道到财务结果全程负责——这正是我想成长为的样子。In past roles, I usually owned one piece of the puzzle. Brand and CBD managers own the whole thing — insight, product, comms, channel and P&L. That's the kind of person I want to become.

好产品不该因为不会表达而被埋没。我想用对人的理解、对内容的审美和对 AI 的熟悉，帮它们建立品牌，再让品牌回到生意本身。 A great product shouldn't get lost just because it doesn't know how to speak up. I want to help good products build real brands — and turn that brand love into a business that lasts.
